World Series

It's true when they say that no one returns from a trip being the same as when as they departed. Travelling doesn't stop when you arrive back home. Everything you've seen and experienced will have it's place and sometimes it makes you see familiar things with fresh eyes. In 'World Series' you'll find a small compilation of moments captured during various trips around the world. Taste the espresso in Rome, let the rhythm of the Flamenco be your guide in Sevilla, meet a goddess on the Acropolis, catch a yellowcab in New York, fly with the brown pelicans in Aruba and finish with a good cigar in Cuba ...
